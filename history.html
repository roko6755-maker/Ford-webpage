How Ford began

Ford Motor Company was founded by Henry Ford and a group of investors in 1903 in Detroit, Michigan. Its first car was the Model A.

The Model T

In 1908, Ford introduced the Model T. It became very popular because it was designed to be practical and affordable. In 1913, Ford began using a moving assembly line to build cars more quickly.

Ford today

Ford grew into an international car company. Today it makes cars, trucks, and electric vehicles, including models such as the Mustang, Bronco, and F-150.
